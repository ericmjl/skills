# Video Retention Science

Distilled 2026-08-26 from the learn-anything founder-video research pass. Use when
writing or auditing VIDEO SCRIPTS (founder videos, landing-page videos, explainers)
or any timed spoken persuasion artifact. Do NOT re-research — the findings are here.

## Hook (first 15-30 seconds)

- More than 55% of drop-off happens in the first 60 seconds (YouTube data).
- PrePublish 349-video study: what separates hits from flops is CONCRETENESS, not
  pace — speaking rate was identical (181 vs 180 wpm) and time-to-point identical
  (word 14 vs 15). Hits open with a concrete claim (33% vs 25%), contain a specific
  number (63% vs 52%), and engage the title's promise (87% vs 78%). Flops open with
  context dumps (26%) or greetings (12%).
- Practical deadline: a concrete value claim within 15 seconds. No "hi, welcome to..."
  openings.

## Open loops

- Loewenstein information-gap theory (1994): curiosity is an aversive drive from a
  specific gap — context must come first to create the gap.
- Zeigarnik effect: open loops sustain attention; interrupted tasks are remembered
  roughly 90% better.
- Loop architecture: one macro-loop spanning the whole video plus micro-loops every
  15-30 seconds; at least one loop always open; resolve ALL loops by the end
  (unresolved loops damage trust).
- Curiosity-gap lifespan: 3-4 minutes.

## Mid-video sag

- Expect an attention dip at 20-35% of the way through. Fix with a re-engagement
  beat: stakes escalation, specificity injection, a new open loop, a perspective
  shift, or social proof.
- Provide a micro-satisfaction roughly every 15 seconds.

## Speech rate

- Rodero 2019 (Journal of Advertising Research): 180 wpm optimal for spoken ads;
  160 wpm worst performance.
- Kim/Allen/Preiss 2019 meta-analysis: moderate-fast speech maximizes persuasion;
  faster speakers are rated more credible/expert. Too slow reduces perceived
  competence.
- Conversational script target: 155-170 wpm.

## CTA and friction

- The conversion goal is a "brainless move" (Eric's spec): one-field signup, button
  immediately below the video, social proof adjacent to the CTA.
- The script's job is to close every open loop INTO the CTA so that signing up
  releases the built tension.
