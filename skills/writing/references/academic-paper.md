# Academic paper route

These skills are **not** in this repository. They come from a separate
clone of [HKUSTDial/Supervisor-Skills](https://github.com/HKUSTDial/Supervisor-Skills)
(Yuyu Luo and contributors), usually symlinked into the global skills
folder. They are not vendored here: that suite is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which
does not match this repo's MIT license.

The suite is separately licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial); flag
that before using it for paid client work.

If the matching skill is not installed, say so, give the upstream URL
above, and fall back to the general writing route in `SKILL.md`. Do not
invent a paper-writing checklist, and do not substitute a different paper
skill because a nearby one happens to be present.

## Which skill to load

Load the matching skill first. If more than one job applies, load each;
do not merge them from memory.

| When the job is | Load |
| --- | --- |
| Judging whether a research idea is worth pursuing | `idea-evaluator` |
| A survey-grade literature review | `deep-research` |
| Planning a methods paper | `tech-paper-template` |
| Planning a benchmark or evaluation paper | `benchmark-paper-template` |
| Drafting the paper's Introduction | `intro-drafter` |
| Drafting other sections, with evidence and checked citations | `paper-writer` |
| Polishing paper prose, calibrating claims, or rewriting Chinese paper prose into English | `paper-polish` |
| Planning figures for a paper | `figure-designer` |
| A reviewer-style audit before submission | `pre-submission-reviewer` |
| Planning a reply to reviewers | `rebuttal-guidance` |

## After the paper skill

This library's general prose skills (`coherent-writing`,
`processing-friendly-prose`, and the rest of the pass order in `SKILL.md`)
may run afterwards as optional passes on prose. They must not override
citation or evidence rules from the paper skills.
